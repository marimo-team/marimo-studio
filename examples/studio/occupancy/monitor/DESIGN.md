# Room 01 field notebook

Present the room's environmental record as a readable field notebook. Use a warm
white page, generous space, serif section headings, and small sans-serif labels.
Evergreen identifies the measured signal, gray identifies its rolling baseline,
and amber identifies flagged readings. Color carries data meaning.

Place scope and signal controls before a compact four-measurement summary. Give
the time series the most space, followed by daily occupancy. Keep source and
interpretation notes at the end. On phones, stack controls and use two columns
for measurements. Plot labels must remain readable and native controls must
retain their full interaction area. On narrow screens, show daily occupancy as
horizontal bars with month-and-day labels and a percentage beside every bar.
Keep the same zero-to-100-percent scale used on desktop.

The Marimo notebook owns analytical definitions and all shared data. Notebook
Kit cells connect projected values to Observable Plot and presentation copy.
